    Overview

    You type a message. The agent figures out what you want, checks that it's clear, then either reads data from the fake bank or prepares a transfer and pauses for your approval. The bank has the final say on money; the agent only tracks where its workflow is up to (graph state saved under a thread_id).
    A restaurant. The waiter (the agent) takes your order, asks if something is unclear, and passes it to the kitchen (the bank). The waiter never cooks and never handles the till.
    You ──► [A] HTTP layer ──► [B] Language understanding ──► [C] The graph (pause / resume) │ [D] Mock bank (truth) ◄┘ [E] Learning tools (UI, eval, tests) watch all of it
